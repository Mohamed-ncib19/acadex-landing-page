Your data

Is my data safe?

Everything stays on your computer, with backups for the day it breaks down.

Stored on your PC

The school's data is saved on your computer's disk. Nothing leaves the machine, except the cloud backup when your pack includes it.

Local backup in every pack

A daily automatic backup set in the settings (the last 7 are kept), manual backups whenever you want, and a one-click restore.

Cloud backup with Plus and Complet

A copy of your data is sent to the cloud from the app, so you can restore it on another computer.

If the PC breaks down

With Plus or Complet, you restore the cloud backup on the new PC. With Essentiel, the backups sit on the same computer: save a copy to a USB key or an external drive regularly. The licence is tied to one PC, so contact us to activate it on the new machine.
